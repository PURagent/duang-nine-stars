2. GeoNames location data

The thai-astrology package adapts provincial-seat and city WGS84 coordinates,
place names, and timezone identifiers from GeoNames. These adapted data are
included with the library. This application uses the library's Thai province
records, rather than precise user/device location or a user's street address.

Attribution: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: selection and adaptation into the thai-astrology data format by the
upstream library; this application does not modify the underlying coordinates.

Original datasets and metadata:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/TH.zip
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
